How to play

Space/LMB holdWeb-swing. Release to launch. Hold toward your arc to pump and gain speed. ADAir control / run. Hold toward a wall while airborne to wall-run up it. SDive. Trade height for speed — the fastest way to build momentum. WWeb-zip. Slingshot toward your anchor point. EAttack. Auto-zips to nearby enemies. From above and unseen: stealth takedown. QDodge when spider-sense tingles — perfect dodge slows time. FWeb-yank an enemy to you (or you to them). RSpend focus to heal. TSuit power. PPhoto mode. EscPause.
Flow tip: dive before each swing, release at the top of the arc, re-web instantly. Never touch the ground.
