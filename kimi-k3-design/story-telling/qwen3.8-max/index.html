Chapter 01 — The problem

We borrowed a world. And spent it like it was ours to burn.

Every year the world cuts, dyes, and stitches a hundred billion garments, then buries most of them within a season. The soil keeps the memory. The rivers keep the color. The nights grow dimmer, and the forests grow thin.

  • 100B garments made each year
  • 92M tonnes of textile waste
  • ×2 production since 2000

We refused to build a house on that ground.

One collection. A whole ecosystem.
