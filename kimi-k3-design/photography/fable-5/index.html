About Elena

Photography is patience, arranged.

For twelve years I've worked with one rule: arrive early, leave late, and let the light decide. My work sits between portraiture and landscape — people in their places, places with a pulse.

Commissions have taken me from the Azores to northern Japan. Prints are made by hand in small editions, on cotton rag, in my Lisbon studio.
