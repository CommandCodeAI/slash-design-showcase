02 — About

I photograph the pause between moments.

I am Elena Voss, a photographer based in Lisbon and working wherever the light asks me to. For twelve years I have made slow, deliberate images — portraits that wait, landscapes that breathe, still lifes of ordinary days.

My practice is analogue at heart: medium format, natural light, very few frames. What I am after is not drama but presence — the instant a face forgets the camera, or a room stops performing.
