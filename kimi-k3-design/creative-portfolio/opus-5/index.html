AboutSince 2019

I design interfaces and then build them, so nothing gets lost in a handoff that never happens.

Most of my week goes into a component library I maintain — 300+ blocks and 12 templates in React and TypeScript, built so a page can be assembled in an afternoon without looking assembled.

The rest goes to client work, single-file HTML5 canvas games I build to stay sharp on rendering loops, and CommandCode, a terminal-based design mode I'm building on top of Claude Code.

Right now I'm pushing further into the backend with Postgres and Prisma, so I can own more of what I ship. I also make videos about all of it.
