(02)

Design-minded engineer, engineer-minded designer. The work lives where the two overlap.

About

I build the kind of websites people remember: fast enough to disappear, considered enough to linger. For seven years I have helped studios, startups, and cultural institutions turn ambitious ideas into interfaces that hold up under real fingers.

My path started in graphic design and detoured through a computer science degree, which is a long way of saying I care equally about how things look and how they are made. I prototype in the browser early, judge work as an experience rather than a screenshot, and treat performance budgets as a design material like color or type.

I work best as a small team's embedded creative developer, taking projects from first sketch to shipped product. No handoff gaps, no lost-in-translation details.

(03)

Four phases, one loop. Every project passes through each, and the last feeds the first.

Process

A project with me is not a black box. This is the shape of the work, in the order it happens.

01

Discover

Before pixels, questions. What is this project really for, who does it serve, and what would make it undeniable? I audit the brand, the audience, and the technical ground until the brief sharpens into a point of view we both sign.

ResearchStrategyScope
02

Design

Art direction and interface design move together. Type, color, motion, and layout are explored as one system, prototyped in the browser early so decisions are judged as experiences, not still images.

Art directionPrototypesDesign systems
03

Develop

Semantic markup, hand-tuned CSS, and JavaScript only where it earns its place. Every animation runs on transform and opacity, every state is built, and every page answers to a performance budget.

ComponentsMotionAccessibility
04

Ship and refine

Launch is the midpoint, not the finish line. I watch how real people use the thing, measure what matters, and iterate until the production experience matches the prototype.

LaunchAnalyticsIteration
