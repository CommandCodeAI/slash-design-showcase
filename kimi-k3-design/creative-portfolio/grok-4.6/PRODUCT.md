# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, design leads, and studio founders reviewing a frontend developer's portfolio at night, on a laptop, often with a tab full of other candidates. They decide in under a minute whether the work feels inevitable. Secondary: peers and collaborators who arrive via Awwwards, social, or a forwarded link.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Noor Elian, an independent frontend artist in Rotterdam. The site is the proof: type, motion, and code sharing one pulse. Success is a visitor who feels the craft before they finish the hero, then books a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Machined, hushed, theatrical. Quiet until it isn't. Confidence without swagger. Copy is short, specific, and never ornamental.

## Anti-references

- Editorial-magazine portfolios: italic display serif, mono labels, ruled columns, cream-on-charcoal
- SaaS-cream landing pages and identical icon-card grids
- Neon-on-black "creative developer" clichés, particle soup, glassmorphism for its own sake
- Dribbble-template heroes: centered headline, three feature cards, rounded screenshots
- Gradient text, side-stripe accents, Inter/Space Grotesk/Fraunces defaults

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: the site is the case study
- Type is the primary object, not a caption for images
- One idea per fold; pacing over density
- Motion must feel physical (weight, friction) or it does not ship
- Remove until the remaining thing is unavoidable

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible `:focus-visible` rings. `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces spatial motion with fades and static type. Custom cursor only on fine pointers. Skip link. Form labels always visible. Touch targets ≥44px. Never lock zoom.
