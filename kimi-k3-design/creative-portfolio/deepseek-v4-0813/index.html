About

I'm Elias. For the past eight years I've sat between design and engineering, building interfaces that are as considered in motion as they are in markup.

My work lives where performance and emotion meet — WebGL scenes that render in a single frame, scroll that feels physical, typography that holds a room. I care about the details users feel but rarely name.

Previously I led frontend at studios in Berlin and London. Now independent, partnering with teams who want the web to feel less like software and more like a place.
