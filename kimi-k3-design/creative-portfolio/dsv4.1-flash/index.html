Prologue

Most portfolios show screens. This one shows the work that made them.

Interfaces are not pictures. They are objects with weight, timing, and consequences. The first two hundred milliseconds decide whether a thing feels alive, and the way a number changes decides whether you trust it. I spend my time there, on the parts that are hard to see and easy to feel.

Five projects below. Each one shipped, and each one broke something on the way.

02 Method

How the work gets made

Four habits that survive contact with a real deadline.

Capabilities

  • Motion systems
  • Design systems
  • Accessibility

  1. 01

    Interrogate

    Before the first line of code I want the constraint that makes this project different from the last one. Usually it is not technical. Usually it is a person, a deadline, or a number nobody has agreed on yet.

  2. 02

    Compose

    Structure first, then rhythm, then surface. Every layout decision is settled before a single colour is chosen, because colour cannot rescue a page that does not know where the eye should land.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-written front-end. No framework I cannot explain to your team in one meeting, and no dependency that outlives the problem it solved.

  4. 04

    Ship

    A performance budget is a design constraint, not a cleanup task. When the budget is gone, the slow thing gets cut rather than optimised later.
