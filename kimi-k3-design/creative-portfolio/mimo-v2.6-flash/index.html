About

Oslo, Norway

For eight years I have worked at the seam between design and engineering: first as a frontend developer in Stockholm studios, now independently from Oslo.

I prototype in the browser, art-direct in Figma, and stay on a project until the last easing curve feels right. The work below is a selection; the rest of the archive is available on request.
