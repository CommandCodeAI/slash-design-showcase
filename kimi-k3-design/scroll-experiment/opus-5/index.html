A career bent by gravity.

First kinetic works exhibited — steel armatures learning to breathe.

Began the generative sound practice that still runs beneath every later piece.

Bronze cast around live circuitry. The metal reads the current.

DIMENSION 04 · FRAGMENTS

I do not build objects. I build thresholds.

Every material remembers the force that shaped it.

Entropy is not decay. It is the material's way of speaking.

Between the physical and the simulated, there is no border — only translation.

My work ends where your attention begins.
